SDUSD FIELD REPORT - INSTALLABLE APP FOR IPHONE AND ANDROID
===========================================================

This folder is a complete phone app. Put it on a website once, and anyone
can install it from a link. It gets the Plant Operations seal as its
home-screen icon, opens full screen like any other app, works offline on
site, and uses the camera, GPS, voice and the phone's share menu.

The same link works on iPhone and Android. There is nothing to approve in
an app store, and updates reach everyone automatically.


STEP 1 - PUT THE FOLDER ONLINE (one time, about 5 minutes, free)
-----------------------------------------------------------------

Use a computer for this step.

Option A: Netlify Drop (easiest)
  1. Unzip the package so you have the folder "sdusd-field-report-app".
  2. Go to https://app.netlify.com/drop and sign up (free).
  3. Drag the whole folder onto the page.
  4. Netlify gives you an address like https://random-name-123.netlify.app
  5. Rename it: Site configuration > Change site name, for example
     https://sdusd-field-report.netlify.app
  6. Claim the site to your account when Netlify asks, so it stays up.

Option B: GitHub Pages (free)
  Upload the folder's contents to a public GitHub repository, then turn on
  Settings > Pages > Deploy from a branch > main > / (root).
  If you connect GitHub in Claude, Claude can do this step for you.

Option C: District web server
  Ask IT to place the folder on any https:// web server. It is only static
  files, with no database or server code.


STEP 2 - INSTALL IT ON YOUR PHONE
---------------------------------

iPhone: open the link in Safari, tap the Share button, then
        "Add to Home Screen", then Add.
Android: open the link in Chrome. Tap "Install" on the banner, or tap the
         three-dot menu, then "Install app".

Open it from the new icon and allow Location and Camera when asked.


STEP 3 - GIVE IT TO YOUR TEAM
-----------------------------

In the app: Settings > Share the app with your team.
  - "Share app link" sends the link by text, email or Teams with install
    instructions included.
  - Coworkers can also scan the QR code on your screen with their phone
    camera.


SENDING REPORTS TO STAKEHOLDERS
-------------------------------

1. Report tab > "Send report to stakeholders" (or History > Send for a past
   inspection).
2. Add stakeholders once: name, email and role. They're saved and picked
   automatically for the same project next time. On Android you can also
   pick from your phone contacts.
3. Choose what to attach:
   - PDF: photos, notes, GPS and a signature line. Best for most people.
   - PDF + Excel: adds a spreadsheet for tracking items.
   - Full package: includes every original photo and video. It's usually
     too big for email, so share it to Google Drive, OneDrive or Teams.
4. The subject and message are written for you: a summary, then the open
   and critical items with who is responsible and due dates. Edit as needed.
5. Tap "Send with attachment", then choose Mail, Outlook, Gmail or Teams.
   The recipient addresses are copied, so paste them into the To line if
   your mail app leaves it blank.

"Summary email" opens your mail app with recipients, subject and message
filled in, without an attachment. Use it for quick updates.

Every send is logged on the inspection, with date and recipients.
The PDF lists everyone on the distribution.


UPDATING THE APP LATER
----------------------
Replace the files on the website with the new version (on Netlify:
Deploys > drag the new folder). Phones pick up the update the next time the
app opens with a connection. Saved inspections and stakeholders are kept.


GOOD TO KNOW
------------
- Inspections, photos and stakeholders stay on each person's own phone.
  Nothing is uploaded anywhere unless someone taps Send or Share.
- Back up weekly: Settings > Back up everything. A backup restores on a new
  phone, including the stakeholder list.
- Always open it from the home-screen icon. A browser tab keeps its own
  separate copy of the data.
- Keep the same web address. If it ever changes, back up first, install
  from the new address, then restore.
- App Store or Google Play listing: possible later, but it needs an Apple
  Developer account ($99/year) and a Google Play account ($25 one time),
  plus store review. Once the app is online, https://www.pwabuilder.com can
  package it for both stores. Installing from the link gives the same app
  without that.
- Before rolling out district-wide, check with IT about approved apps and
  how photos of school sites should be stored and shared.
